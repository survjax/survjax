D-043: Correct the nested custom-condition expectation

Maintainer approved this correction on 2026-11-06.

In tests/spec/test_a11_ph_parameter_issues.py, update
  test_custom_baseline_condition_appears_with_the_prefix
so baseline.rate_above_cap expects [False, True, True], rather than
[False, True, False].

The custom CappedRate family returns rate > cap. For rate=[1,3,1] and
cap=[2,2,-1], the last comparison is 1 > -1, hence True. Spec 4.7 requires
preserving that condition mask, independently of the invalid-cap field mask.
Keep baseline.cap=[False,False,True] and invalid_entries=[False,True,True].

No spec, implementation, custom-family predicate or tolerance change is needed.
Rerun the affected test in separate processes with JAX_ENABLE_X64=1 and 0.
Commit the correction in the independent spec-test repo and hand it back
through the normal spec-test synchronization workflow.

## Mathematical origin and invariants

- PH Exponential has natural rate r*exp(eta), because H=exp(eta)*r*t.
  PH Weibull has shape k and scale b*exp(-eta/k), because that gives exactly
  exp(eta)*(t/b)**k. Differentiating the equivalent scale includes the chain
  rule in k as well as b and eta; all natural baseline fields are tested.
- References for the method identities are the separately implemented built-in
  methods at those analytically transformed parameters. This is the precise
  equivalence demanded by spec 4.4, not an expected fixture generated by PH.
  Independent hand-derived Hessians provide another source of expected values.
- All Jacobian points have positive finite times and valid smooth interior
  parameters. Zero/infinity grid cases assert values only. No density/hazard
  derivatives across Weibull's k=1 boundary discontinuity are promised.
- Scalar baseline/predictor plus three times gives three values. Mapping two
  root parameter draws gives (2,3). The broadcast cases use baseline (3,1)
  and eta (3,2), giving batch (3,2). Grids are (4,3,2) at four limit/interior
  times and (2,3,2) for interior Jacobians. Paired time (2,) returns (3,2),
  deliberately distinct from the K=N=2 grid. Likelihood keeps (3,2).
- Every numeric allclose in this valid-input suite has equal_nan=False;
  matching NaNs cannot make an identity or derivative check pass. The only
  allowed nonfinite values are mathematical direct/grid boundary infinities.
- The test helpers construct both sides from the original input fields. They
  do not use PH closure/delegation or any private numerical helper. Production
  direct methods and likelihood continue to use PH formulas.
- No source, exports, docs, appendix, dependencies, protected paths, fixture or
  tolerance changes. Exactly 260 new test lines, plus plan/review records.

## Tests and requirements

- test_direct_identity_values_and_first_derivatives (14 cases): spec 4.4/7.3;
  both families × all seven direct methods. Eager/jit values, jit-of-vmap
  parameter draws and jit forward/reverse parameter Jacobians. Positive
  interior times avoid relying on undefined density/hazard boundary derivatives.
- test_grid_identity_broadcast_limits_and_field_derivatives (14 cases): spec
  4.4–4.5/5; all seven grid twins, scalar-time vmap versus grids, eager/jit,
  paired versus shared K=N times, and separate Jacobian leaves for every
  original broadcast input. Weibull rows include k=.7, 1 and 1.7, so density/
  hazard zero/infinity limits differ across rows and are compared with the
  analytical equivalent family. Derivative grids contain only interior times.
- test_likelihood_identity_both_forms_and_broadcast_jacobians (2 cases): spec
  4.4/6.1/7.3; prepared/raw exact/right inputs, eager/jit/vmap over draws,
  preserved contribution shape and forward/reverse Jacobians in each field.
- test_likelihood_hessian_matches_hand_derivation (2 cases): spec 7.3/12.1;
  for data times (.5,1.5,2), events (1,0,1), the mean Exponential log L is
  (2/3)*(log(r)+eta)-(4/3)*r*exp(eta). Its matrix is differentiated explicitly.
  For Weibull, u=log(t/b), H=exp(eta+k*u), c=2/3 and
  L=c*(log(k)-log(b)+eta)+(k-1)*mean(event*u)-mean(H).
  The code states its independent second derivatives using W=mean(H),
  A=mean(H*u), B=mean(H*u*u). Both mixed AD orders match these hand matrices;
  check_grads additionally checks order 2 in forward and reverse mode, with
  existing central O(1) finite-difference scales. No tolerances were widened.
- test_weibull_zero_grid_survival_has_zero_shape_derivative: spec 4.5/7.3;
  independent S(0)=1 identity and exact zero shape derivatives at k=.7, 1,
  1.7 on a grid starting at zero, jit/vmap and both AD modes.

# Mathematical derivations

These notes explain the arithmetic behind specific implementations. For usage,
start with [proportional hazards](../proportional-hazards.md) or [RMST](../rmst.md).

| Topic | Derivation | Source |

The PH note explains stable scaling in log space. The RMST note derives the
series, approximation bounds and safe arithmetic used across its numerical
regions. [RMST numerics and derivatives](rmst.md) supplies executable examples
of the accuracy domain and derivative restrictions.

These are the existing repository appendices, linked here without duplicating
their contents. Their {download}`README <../../appendices/README.md>` records
how to rebuild the PDFs when the mathematical sources change.
